# BR-024 — you were answered, and then two boxes sat unticked for a day

The banner at the top of your document was written on 2026-09-14 about the **three routes**, and it
quietly stood in for the whole request. Two acceptance items were about *pages* rather than code,
and nobody ticked them. Both are closed now.

## 1 · `verification.md` named a code that can never arrive as `error.code`

Your own ⚠ found this and left it in place. The row said:

```
| 404 | KYC_SUBJECT_NOT_FOUND | No such vendor / agency / agent … |
```

`KYC_SUBJECT_NOT_FOUND` is declared in `jovi-mall/src/core/error-codes.ts:1329`, in **neither** of
wi-admin's registries, and this read is delegated — `verificationRecord` calls `platformRequest`
and does not remap. So it arrives as `PLATFORM_OPERATION_REJECTED` (status 404, category
`not_found`) with the real code in `details.platformCode`. **A client branching on `error.code`
would never match it**, exactly as you said.

The table now names `PLATFORM_OPERATION_REJECTED` in the `error.code` column and puts
`KYC_SUBJECT_NOT_FOUND` where it actually lands, with the reason on the row. It is also
catalogued in [`errors.md`](../../api/errors.md) now.

## 2 · `agencies.md` showed four `kyc` members and the DTO emits six

Confirmed at source, `agency.controller.ts:175-192`. The two missing are the two that matter:

| Field | Why it is not optional for a reviewer |
|---|---|
| `kyc.status` | **The verdict.** Not derivable from the agency's own `status` — `pending_verification` is where an agency sits *both* before a review and after a refused one |
| `kyc.rejectionReason` | Set on `rejected`, **and shown to the agency**, who has to know what to fix |

Both are now in the worked JSON and in the field notes, each carrying its own reason.

**Your diagnosis of the consequence is the durable part, and it is now on the page too.** This
dashboard transcribed a page that lagged its service, so `AgencyKyc` declared four fields and the
review dialog re-derived the verdict from `verified` + `agency.status` — **reproducing precisely
the ambiguity the backend had already removed.** A re-applying agency read as one nobody had
looked at, and the sentence they had been given was nowhere on the screen.

## ⚠ One behaviour you did not find, and it will affect the card

**The three routes are not on `ONBOARDING_ROUTE_ALLOWLIST`**, so a `pending` administrator gets
`403 ADMIN_ACTIVATION_REQUIRED` on all three.

That is **correct rather than an oversight, and the reason is worth knowing**: a verified phone is
*not* part of activation. `employee-readiness.ts` requires **a phone number on the employee
record** — gap code `phone_missing`, section `contact` — and never consults `phone_verified`. So
the card must not render during onboarding, and "verify your phone" is not a step an administrator
can be asked to complete before their account is `active`.

# BR-025 § 2 — done, and the asymmetry beside it is now a decision rather than a leftover

`platformcode` is on `RATE_LIMIT_DETAIL_KEYS`. A forwarded 429 now carries
`details.platformCode`, so your two remedies are finally distinguishable:

| `details.platformCode` | What it means | Remedy |
|---|---|---|
| `PHONE_VERIFICATION_RESEND_TOO_SOON` | Cooldown. The code already in their hand **still works** | Wait `details.retryAfterSeconds` |
| `PHONE_VERIFICATION_TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS` | **The code has been destroyed** | Request a new one |

**Your argument is the one that carried it** — `platformCode` passes the same one test the 5xx
branch states, on the identical key, and nothing about a 429 makes a published error code more
sensitive than it is at 502. You can delete the both-remedies-at-once notice in `PhoneNumberCard`.

⛔ **403 was deliberately NOT changed, and please do not read that as an oversight to fix later.**
A 403 is the one place where naming what refused tells a caller what to go after next, and no
screen is broken for want of it. **The asymmetry between the two is now the decision.** It is
written in three places so it cannot drift back into looking accidental:

- the allowlist itself in `detail-policy.ts`, at the key;
- [`errors.md`](../../api/errors.md) § *What travels in `details`*, which said the opposite and was
  marked **"Verified 2026-09-08"** when it did;
- `test:contract` § 11, which gained **three** assertions — a 429 keeps `platformCode`,
  `retryAfterSeconds` travels *beside* it rather than instead of it, and **a 403 still drops it**.
  A later tidy-up in either direction turns one of them red.

⚠ **This was never phone-specific.** It is every delegated 429 on the service; your flow is simply
where two refusals first wanted contradictory advice.

## Also now catalogued: the six `PHONE_VERIFICATION_*` codes

[`errors.md`](../../api/errors.md) § *"The six that arrive from jovi-mall"* lists all six with the
`details` each carries. One you may not have: **`PHONE_VERIFICATION_CODE_INVALID` carries
`details.attemptsLeft`**, and it survives to you. It is disclosed deliberately — it tells the
holder of the real code that they mistyped and how much room they have, and it tells an attacker
something they could count themselves. The secret is the code, not the counter.

# BR-025 § 3 — recorded, and **the premise underneath it is false**

This is the part of the round worth reading twice.

## The message really does not survive, and that is now written down

Your § 3 is correct: `PHONE_VERIFICATION_DELIVERY_FAILED` is raised at 502, wi-admin remaps it to
`SERVICE_DEPENDENCY_UNAVAILABLE`, `platformCode` survives, and `projectMessage` replaces the
sentence with the registry default. jovi-mall's promise that *"the message names the template"* is
true only on its own boundary. Both pages say so now. Nothing changes.

## ⛔ But "the flow self-heals the day `wi_mall_phone_verification` is approved" is not true

> ### ✅ RETRACTED LATER THE SAME DAY (2026-09-15) — YOUR § 3 WAS RIGHT
>
> **That day was the day.** The owning business reached `business_verification_status:
> "verified"`, `wi_mall_phone_verification` was submitted, and Meta **approved both languages
> within seconds** (`4426347317613316` en, `1393590468966788` fr). The flow self-healed with **no
> code change and no redeploy**, precisely as your § 3 said it would — the send path had always
> tried AUTHENTICATION first.
>
> ⚠ **Read the retraction narrowly: the measurement below was correct, the forecast was not.**
> The template genuinely could not be created that morning, for the reason stated, and knowing
> that reason is what made the recovery a single command once the verification landed. What was
> wrong was turning "blocked today, on someone else's review queue" into "there is no such day".
>
> ⛔ **Still true, and the reason it is worth keeping this page:**
> `wi_mall_phone_verification_utility` remains rejected and must never be resubmitted.
> `INCORRECT_CATEGORY` is a verdict on OTP **content**; the business verification did not touch
> it, and no amount of rewording is an acceptable answer to it.
>
> **Build the ordinary flow.** The workaround in the next section is now a fallback, not the
> primary path. Current state: [`auth.md` § Delivery](../../api/auth.md).

**There is no such day. That template cannot be created at all.**

| Template | Outcome |
|---|---|
| `wi_mall_phone_verification` (AUTHENTICATION) | **Cannot be created** — code 10 / subcode 2388185. Meta gates the category behind business verification and this WABA's owning business is `business_verification_status: "rejected"`. **Not the token** — UTILITY creates fine on the same credential, which is what isolates it |
| `wi_mall_phone_verification_utility` (UTILITY) | **Created, then REJECTED at review in minutes** — `INCORRECT_CATEGORY`, both languages. Resubmitted with `allow_category_change: true`, which lets Meta pick a category rather than refuse: **rejected synchronously** |

**The one real fix is resolving the WhatsApp business verification.** After that the first path
resumes on its own — the send order never changed, so there is no code change and no redeploy.

⛔ **Rewording the OTP copy to get past the classifier is not on the table.** Meta classifies OTP
content as AUTHENTICATION and accepts it nowhere else, so rewording until the classifier stops
recognising it is evading enforcement rather than satisfying it — and the WABA carrying the
platform's other 188 templates is the stake.

## ✅ There is a workaround that works today, and it is worth putting in front of the operator

In-window delivery needs no template, because a free-form message is allowed. And the window is
keyed on **the phone number alone** — `open_chat_window:<waPhoneId>`, no account, no user id. So:

> **Send any WhatsApp message to the platform's business number from the phone being verified,
> then press Verify within the next 23 hours.** The code arrives as ordinary text.

**Keeping the Verify button enabled was the right call** — for a better reason than the one you
gave. Two caveats to state honestly if you surface this:

- **23 hours, not 24.** The key's TTL is deliberately short of Meta's real boundary so free-form
  sends stop with a margin rather than racing it.
- **Messaging the bot creates a customer account against that phone number**, via `/identity/sync`.
  Harmless to the administrator record — administrator identity lives in wi-admin's own database
  and the OTP subject is namespaced `admin:<id>` precisely so the two cannot collide — but the row
  does come into existence, and an operator should not discover that by accident.

⚠ Your observation that this refusal is *"the ordinary case for an administrator today, not the
edge one"* is exactly right, and `auth.md` now says so in those terms under
[*Why verification usually fails today*](../../api/auth.md#why-verification-usually-fails-today).
